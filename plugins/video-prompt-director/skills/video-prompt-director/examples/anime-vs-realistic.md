# One beat, two looks

The same 6 s beat written for the realistic mode and the anime mode (`references/anime-direction.md`). All material is original. Apply the target model's syntax and limits from `references/models/` before use.

The beat: a courier reaches a rooftop door at dusk, stops, and looks back at the city.

## Realistic

> Medium shot, eye level, locked-off camera, 50 mm look, shallow depth of field, warm low sun from camera left. A courier in a green rain jacket steps onto a concrete rooftop and stops at a metal door, her right hand resting on the handle. Her breath shows slightly in the cold air. She turns her head over her left shoulder and looks at the city for a moment, her brows lifting slightly. She ends facing the door, hand on the handle, head turned toward the skyline.

## Anime

> 2D hand-drawn anime look: thin dark-tone outlines of steady weight, flat cel-shaded color with one hard-edged shadow tone per surface, painted background with soft sunset gradients, animated on twos. Medium shot, eye level, locked-off camera. A courier with a short black bob, amber eyes and a green rain jacket steps onto a rooftop and stops at a metal door, her right hand on the handle. A hard orange rim light outlines her hair and shoulder from camera left. She holds the pose while her hair and the jacket hem lift in the wind. At 3 s she turns her head over her left shoulder; her eyes widen a little and a bright glint crosses them. The painted skyline stays still behind her. She ends facing the door, hand on the handle, head turned toward the skyline. Consistent line weight and face design throughout; no on-screen text.

## What changed and why

- The lens and depth-of-field words became painted-background and line words. Photographic terms do not belong in the anime prompt.
- The character is a design (hair, eyes, jacket) that can be repeated word for word, not a photographic description.
- Wind in hair and cloth carries the held pose, so the body does not need extra action.
- The beat has one action and one reaction in both versions. The style changed; the plan did not.
