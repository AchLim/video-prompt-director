## Documented capabilities (verified 2026-11-11)

**Gemini Omni Flash 1.2 (`gemini-omni-1.2-flash`), Gemini API**
- Inputs: text, image and video.
- Duration: whole seconds from 3 to 15, default 10.
- Aspect ratio: default 16:9; `9:16` gives portrait.
- Resolution: default 720p; 360p, 1080p (upscaled) and 4K (upscaled) are options.
- References: up to 6 reference images per request, up to 3 images per character for up to 2 characters. Reference videos are allowed.
- First and last frame: supply two images in the input list; limited to 3 to 10 seconds. Extension is 3 to 10 seconds and can reach 30 seconds total.
- Audio: generated by default; describe it in the prompt. Voice editing and audio inputs are not supported. Spoken dialogue is supported when extending model-generated videos; you cannot extend an uploaded video to add dialogue where someone is already talking.
- Prompt length limit: not documented.
- Negative prompt parameter: not supported. Write exclusions in the prompt.
- Language: English fully supported; other languages "may work".

**Flow**
- Omni Flash 1.2 without a subscription: text to video, frames to video, ingredients (references) to video, video to video; 3 to 10 seconds. With a paid Google AI plan: the same at 3 to 15 seconds, plus Omni Extend.
- Veo 3.1 Lite and Fast: text to video and frames to video at 4, 6 or 8 seconds; ingredients 8 seconds only; no video to video or extend.
- Veo 3.1 Quality: text to video and frames at 4, 6 or 8 seconds; ingredients not supported.
- Omni Flash 1.1: 4, 6, 8 or 10 seconds.
- Veo 3.1 Fast, Veo 3.1 Quality and Omni Flash 1.1 are listed as scheduled for retirement on December 5, 2026.
- Reference-image counts for Flow ingredients, and audio on Veo in Flow: not documented on the page read.

**Vertex AI:** for Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, 3 to 15 seconds and 360p, 720p, 1080p or 4K. Veo-specific durations, resolutions and aspect ratios on Vertex: not documented on pages opened.

## Prompting guidance from the official pages

- Ask for a "single unbroken or continuous shot" to avoid default cuts (Omni page).
- Use specific camera, subject-motion and environment descriptions rather than "make it move".
- Timing can be written as "After 3 seconds..." or `[0-5s]`.
- Describe each input's role in plain language.
- The Vertex guide lists prompt elements: subject, action, scene and context, camera angle, camera movement, lens effects, style, temporal elements, audio, and negative prompts. It says not every element is needed in every prompt, and that some advanced angles and lenses are not officially supported.
- Audio: say whether you want it, and write sound effects, ambient noise and dialogue in separate sentences. Dialogue is written inline, for example: the man in the red hat says: Where is the rabbit?
- Exclusions: the two Google pages disagree. The Vertex guide advises describing what you do not want rather than using "no" or "don't". The Omni page suggests direct exclusions such as "No dialogue". If one phrasing fails, try the other.

## Production note (one team's log, not from the official pages)

A prompt that compared a character's look to real stars or popular dramas was rejected by Flow as depicting a prominent person (2026-10-31). Describe faces, skin, hair and light directly instead (`references/constraints.md` section 12).

## How the skill applies this


- Name the model and surface before writing limits into a prompt.
- For low-motion drama on Omni Flash 1.2, load `references/models/omni-flash-drama.md`: it applies these limits (15 s, 10 s with two keyframes, 6 reference images) as guardrails.
- Ask for one continuous shot unless the storyboard wants cuts.
- Use `[0-5s]` or "After 3 seconds" beats; this model family documents them.
- Put dialogue inline with the speaker named, and ask first whether audio should be generated. Audio is on by default for Omni Flash, so if the user gave no audio direction, add no sound-effect or music lines and say in a note that audio will be generated unless they want it described or excluded.
- Write exclusions inside the prompt.
- Keep clips to the documented duration of the chosen model and mode.
